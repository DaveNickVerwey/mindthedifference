Mind the Difference Challenge

Voor Nederlandse en internationale studenten. Lees deze pagina in het Nederlands (Nederlandse versie van deze pagina).

What if you could change something that not everyone can take part in right now? In the Challenge you develop an idea of your own, on your own or with a small team. In March 2027 you pitch it at the Mind the Difference Showcase. Maybe your idea will become reality after that!

What's in it for you?

  • Experience with a real-world question.
  • New people around you: students and professionals from outside your own field.
  • Contact with organisations in the cultural sector.
  • Your own pitch at the Showcase.
  • Proof of participation, for your CV or LinkedIn.

Your Challenge in four questions

You develop your idea using four questions. You answer the same questions in your submission.

  1. What's the problem?

    Pick one barrier from your own field, for example in a class, a product, a place or a rule. Who can't take part right now, and what is stopping them?

  2. Who do you need to involve?

    Talk to people who face the barrier themselves, for example fellow students or people in your network or neighbourhood. What do they experience? And what helps them? Don't decide for someone else what they need: find out together.

  3. What's your idea?

    Work out an idea together that can really make a difference. How could you try it out? It doesn't have to be perfect yet.

  4. What's different now?

    Tell us what you discovered along the way. What surprised you? And what will you do differently from now on?

Your starting point

You choose:

  • Something from your own field that not everyone can take part in right now. Were you at the masterclass? Then your card from 25 November can help you.
  • Or the InCultuur case: how can we help disabled and non-disabled visitors meet each other more often? You can link this case to No Limits Festival, Holland Dance Festival or The Grey Space in the Middle.

In a team or on your own

Ideally, work with two or three people from different courses. Different perspectives are exactly what makes your idea stronger. Someone who wasn't at the masterclass can join too. They just need to sign up themselves. Looking for teammates, or does your team have room for someone? Say so when you sign up, and we'll help you find each other.

Rather work on your own? Fine! Do involve people who face the barrier themselves.

What do you send in?

You send in your developed idea by Monday 1 March 2027 at the latest, with your answers to the four questions. You choose the form yourself, for example a video, a reel, a visual concept, a prototype or a written text. Your idea doesn't have to be perfect or finished, and you don't need a project plan. We'll email you later about how to send it in.

The Showcase

March is your moment! In a short pitch you present your idea to experts by experience, professionals from the cultural sector and the City of The Hague. Is your idea new and promising? Then we'll explore together whether it can become reality. And of course the idea remains yours!
